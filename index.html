1. CORE CONCEPT

This sensor detects "Physical Boundaries" (Geometry), not Qualia. It acts as a gatekeeper to reject corrupted data before human review.

2. DECISION CODES

  • CUT CRITICAL FAILURE: Immediate rejection required. (e.g., Clipping > 0.1%, No numbers in spec)
  • HOLD WARNING: Boundary violation detected. Human verification needed. (e.g., Suspicious dynamics, Missing responsibility clauses)
  • PASS CLEAN: Data is within the defined physical safety protocol.

3. PRESETS

  • STRICT: For Broadcast/Finance. Zero tolerance.
  • DEFAULT: Standard Production balance.
  • LENIENT: For Demos/Roughs. Prevents only fatal errors.
